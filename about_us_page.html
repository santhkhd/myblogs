India's Fast, Free & Verified Government Recruitment, Examination & Career Information Network

Our Mission & Purpose

Welcome to 1IndiaJob (accessible at 1indiajob.blogspot.com). Our primary goal is to provide honest, timely, simplified, and verified recruitment information to job aspirants across all 28 Indian States and 8 Union Territories.

Whether you are preparing for Kerala PSC, Staff Selection Commission (SSC), Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB), Banking (IBPS/SBI), UPSC, Defence (Army/Navy/Air Force), or State Government recruitments, we aggregate and structure official notices into easy-to-read job summaries with direct official application portals.

Strict Editorial & Verification Standards

Every employment notification, syllabus breakdown, answer key, and result published on 1IndiaJob goes through a rigorous multi-step manual verification process:

  • Official Gazette Sourcing: We only track official websites of central government ministries, state public service commissions, employment news bulletins, and recognized public sector undertakings (PSUs).
  • Direct Primary Links: We always supply the direct, genuine application and notification PDF links from official government domains (e.g., .gov.in, .nic.in, .in).
  • No Misleading Information: We do not charge fees, collect applicant resumes, or solicit money from job seekers. All content is 100% free for educational and employment guidance.
