📋 Standard Government Age Limits & Category Relaxations

  • Other Backward Classes (OBC - NCL): 3 Years relaxation in upper age limit across all Central and State government jobs.
  • Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes (SC / ST): 5 Years relaxation in upper age limit across SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB, and State PSCs.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD): 10 Years relaxation (PwBD + OBC = 13 Years; PwBD + SC/ST = 15 Years).
  • Ex-Servicemen (ESM): 3 Years after deduction of the military service rendered from the actual age as on the closing date.
  • Kerala PSC General Maximum Age: Standard 18 to 36 years (OBC upper limit: 39 years; SC/ST upper limit: 41 years).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the standard cut-off reference date for SSC and UPSC exams?

SSC and UPSC exams typically set the reference cut-off date as 1st August or 1st January of the examination notification year. Always verify with the official notification.

2. How is age calculated accurately in Leap Years?

Our algorithm accounts for exact calendar month lengths (28, 29, 30, and 31 days) to deliver accurate day, month, and year counts matching official recruitment boards.

3. Can EWS candidates claim upper age relaxation?

Under Central government guidelines, Economically Weaker Section (EWS) candidates receive reservation in vacancies and fee concessions, but not upper age relaxation (unless specifically notified by State governments).
