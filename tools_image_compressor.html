📋 Standard Government Examination KB Size Limits

  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS): Photograph 20 KB to 50 KB; Signature 10 KB to 20 KB.
  • Union Public Service Commission (UPSC Civil Services, NDA, CDS): Photo & Signature 20 KB to 300 KB.
  • Banking & IBPS (PO, Clerk, SO, SBI): Photograph 20 KB to 50 KB; Signature 10 KB to 20 KB; Thumb Impression 20 KB to 50 KB.
  • Kerala PSC Thulasi OTR: Photograph strictly under 30 KB; Signature strictly under 30 KB.
  • Railway Recruitment Board (RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP): Photograph 20 KB to 50 KB; Signature 10 KB to 40 KB.
  • Document Certificates (SSLC, Caste, Degree): Under 200 KB or 500 KB in JPG or PDF format.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How does this tool compress images to an exact KB without blurriness?

Our algorithm uses iterative high-precision JPEG quantization curves directly inside HTML5 Canvas, preserving sharp edges on portraits and text while stripping unnecessary metadata.

2. Can I compress PNG and WebP files into JPG under 20KB or 50KB?

Yes! Any PNG, JPEG, or WebP image selected will be converted into an optimized, standard JPG format required by recruitment exam application forms.

3. Is my uploaded image stored anywhere?

No. All processing is 100% client-side inside your browser. No files or personal data are ever uploaded to or stored on any external server.
