❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why is white background important for exam signatures?

Exam boards like SSC, Kerala PSC, and IBPS use automated OCR systems. Grey paper shadows can cause OCR failure and application rejection. Our tool automatically converts paper background to pure white.

2. Which ink color should I use for signatures?

Black or blue ink on clean unruled white paper is recommended. You can choose black or blue enhancement in step 3.

3. Is my signature kept private?

Absolutely. 100% of processing is done locally inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas. No image data is transmitted to any server.
