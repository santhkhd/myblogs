📋 Government Examination PDF Document Upload Guidelines

  • Standard Size Limit: Must be under 200 KB for State PSCs, SSC, and Banking portals (under 500 KB or 1 MB for UPSC).
  • Legibility: Certificate numbers, university seals, candidate name, date of birth, and marks tables must remain razor-sharp.
  • Multi-Page Merging: All pages of semester marksheets or consolidated transcripts should be merged chronologically into a single PDF document.
  • Orientation: A4 Portrait standard for degree/provisional certificates; Landscape for consolidated marks cards.
  • Privacy Protection: Ensure no password protection is attached to the uploaded PDF files.

1. How can I merge multiple certificate pages into a single PDF under 200 KB?

Upload all your certificate photos, select the 'High Compression (Target < 200 KB)' preset, and click Convert. Our built-in compressor automatically optimizes each page for government portals.
