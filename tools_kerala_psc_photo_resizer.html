📋 Official Kerala PSC Photograph Upload Guidelines

  • Dimensions: 150 pixels width by 200 pixels height (aspect ratio 3:4).
  • File Size: Strictly maximum 30 KB (Our tool optimizes between 15 KB and 28 KB).
  • File Format: JPG / JPEG.
  • Bottom White Banner: The name of the candidate in bold capital letters and the date on which the photograph was taken must be firmly printed in black color at the bottom portion of the photograph.
  • Validity: The photograph once uploaded on Kerala PSC Thulasi One Time Registration is valid for 10 years from the date of taking.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why is Kerala PSC rejecting my uploaded photograph?

Rejections usually occur if the file size exceeds 30 KB, dimensions are not 150×200 px, or candidate name & date taken are missing from the bottom white strip.

2. Is my photo uploaded to any external server?

No. All processing happens 100% locally inside your web browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your photograph is never uploaded to any remote server, ensuring complete data security.
