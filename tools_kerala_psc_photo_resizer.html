❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why does Kerala PSC Thulasi reject my photo?

Common reasons include file size exceeding 30 KB, incorrect 150x200 pixel dimensions, or missing candidate name and date stamp on the bottom white strip.

2. What is the validity of the uploaded photograph?

Photographs uploaded to Kerala PSC Thulasi OTR are valid for 10 years from the date of capture printed on the photo.

3. Is it safe to upload my photo on this tool?

Yes! 100% of all processing and cropping happens directly inside your web browser. Nothing is ever sent to any remote server.
