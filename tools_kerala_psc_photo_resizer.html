📋 Official Kerala PSC Photograph Upload Specifications

  • Dimensions: 150 pixels width by 200 pixels height (aspect ratio 3:4).
  • File Size: Strictly under 30 KB (Optimized automatically between 20 KB and 28 KB).

1. Why does Kerala PSC Thulasi reject my photo?

Common reasons include size exceeding 30 KB, incorrect 150x200 pixel aspect ratio, or missing candidate name and date stamp on the bottom strip.

2. Is it safe to upload my photo here?

Yes! 100% of all processing and cropping happens directly inside your web browser. Nothing is ever sent to any remote server.
